# Episode plan — Devī Māhātmyam (arc 1 of the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa)

Story order. Chapters = Durgā Saptaśatī adhyāyas (= Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa cantos 81–93, Pargiter). Pace: **one episode a
week**. Each episode ≈ 9 narrated beats + hold, ≈ 110–120 s, Orion only, ~9 plates + poster on SuperGrok consumer
Imagine. Primary sources for every episode: Gita Press Gorakhpur *Shri Durga Saptashati*; Swami Jagadiswarananda,
*Devi-Mahatmyam* (Ramakrishna Math); F. E. Pargiter, *The Markandeya Purana* (1904).

Notes
- The Devi appears as a person first in Ep 03; in Ep 01–02 she is formless light / Yoga-nidra (no Devi lock needed).
- Gore policy applies to every plate: battles are shown as light, gestures, roaring lions and fleeing armies; defeats as
  stillness, smoke or light — never wounds, blood or heads (Ep 05, 09, 10 especially).
- Later arcs of the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa (e.g. Harishchandra, Madalasa, the Manvantaras) are out of scope until Avinash picks one.
