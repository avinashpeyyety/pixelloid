# Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa — locked character sheets (Devī Māhātmyam arc)

Source of truth: `config/cast-locks.json` (generated by the box builder). Format matches the Mahabharata cast sheets (face / hair / costume) plus a **never** (drift / hard-fail) list. Lock image path: `episodes/<first-ep>/stills/_locks/<id>.jpg`; later episodes reuse that lock byte-for-byte.

**Tone:** respectful, devotional, child-appropriate. No blood, gore, severed heads or skulls anywhere in the series. Kali wears a garland of red hibiscus flowers; Raktabija's copies sprout from red sparks; Mahishasura is subdued under the trident with no wound shown.

## Markandeya (Mārkaṇḍeya) — `markandeya`

- **Role:** Narrator-sage of the Purana; frames every arc · first episode: Ep 01
- **Face:** Ageless radiant sage who looks about forty (blessed to stay young), warm brown skin, calm large dark eyes, short neat dark-brown beard, three horizontal white ash lines (tripundra) with a small red dot on the forehead
- **Hair:** Long dark-brown matted hair (jata) coiled in a high topknot bound with one rudraksha string; no crown
- **Costume:** Bare chest with a long rudraksha mala, ochre-saffron dhoti and ochre shawl over the left shoulder, holds an open palm-leaf manuscript; barefoot
- **Never:** white beard or white hair (that is Medhas), crown, jewels, weapons, elderly frail body, extra arms

## Medhas (Medhā), the sage — `medhas`

- **Role:** Forest sage who tells the Devi Mahatmyam to Suratha and Samadhi · first episode: Ep 01
- **Face:** Elderly kind sage about seventy, fair-wheatish skin, gentle smiling eyes with laugh lines, long snow-white beard to the chest, vertical white-and-red sandal-paste tilak
- **Hair:** Long flowing snow-white hair to the shoulders with a small white topknot; no crown
- **Costume:** Bare chest with a tulsi-bead mala, plain cream-white cotton dhoti and cream shawl, sits on a spotted deerskin; brass kamandalu (water pot) and a wooden staff nearby; barefoot
- **Never:** dark hair, ochre-saffron robes (that is Markandeya), crown, jewels, weapons, stern or angry face, extra arms

## King Suratha (Suratha) — `suratha`

- **Role:** Good king of the Chaitra line who loses his kingdom; later the Manu Savarni · first episode: Ep 01
- **Face:** Dignified king in his late thirties, wheat-brown skin, strong jaw, thick black upturned mustache, clean-shaven chin (no beard), honest worried dark eyes
- **Hair:** Shoulder-length wavy black hair under a tall gold crown (mukuta) set with one red ruby
- **Costume:** Royal indigo-blue angavastram (upper cloth) over a bare chest with a pearl necklace, gold earrings and armlets, cream silk dhoti with a gold border, gold-hilted sword sheathed at the waist; rides a white horse with a red-and-gold harness
- **Never:** beard, missing crown, armor helmet, drawn sword, saffron sage robes, extra arms

## The Devi — Durga / Ambika / Chandika, as Mahishasuramardini — `devi`

- **Role:** The Great Goddess; heroine of all three charitras · first episode: Ep 03

- **Costume:** Rich red silk sari with a broad gold border, gold necklaces, armlets and anklets; exactly TEN arms (five on each side, symmetric) holding the gods' gifts: trident (Shiva), discus (Vishnu), conch (Varuna), bow and arrows (Vayu), thunderbolt (Indra), sword and shield (Kala), mace, noose, and a lotus; rides a golden-maned lion (gift of Himavan), seated sideways or standing on it
- **Never:** blood, wounds, severed heads, angry snarl or bared teeth, revealing or sexualized clothing, missing or extra arms, black skin (that is Kali), photoreal
