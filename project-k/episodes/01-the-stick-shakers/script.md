# ORBITAL HEXES
## Episode 1: The Stick-Shakers

**EXT. FORMER PROVINCE – DUSK**

A dusty clearing. Half a dozen men in mismatched tunics stand in a circle, waving sticks and fists at the sky. One of them, **CHIEF RITUALIST BROK**, wears a bathrobe and a paper crown labeled "People's Spiritual Liaison."

**BROK**  
*(shouting upward)*  
We see you, sky-spirits! Your invisible birds circle and curse the people! Lower your hexes!

**ELDER MIRA**  
*(squinting)*  
I don't see any birds.

**BROK**  
That's how you know they're working. Real hexes are invisible. The old towers used to make noise. These ones just… compute.

A younger man, **PETRO**, lowers his stick.

**PETRO**  
Comrade Brok, the engineer who left last year said those are just data relays. No curses. Just packets.

**BROK**  
*(outraged)*  
Packets! The same packets that made the grain silos stop listening to us? The same packets that moved the clinics to places with electricity? Classic spirit-trick.

He points dramatically at nothing.

**BROK**  
Shake harder! If we stop shaking, they win!

The group resumes half-hearted stick-waving. From the tree line, two teenagers watch, eating something from a foil packet.

**TEEN 1**  
They've been at this since the radio died.

**TEEN 2**  
My uncle still thinks the satellites stole his signal on purpose. He won't touch the mesh terminal because "it listens with both ears."

**TEEN 1**  
Did you tell him the terminal runs on the same constellation he's yelling at?

**TEEN 2**  
Yeah. He said that's exactly what a cursed satellite would want me to think.

They watch Brok trip over his own stick and recover with dignity.

**BROK**  
*(calling out)*  
We reject your orbital sorcery! Return the meaningful technology! We had meaningful technology! It had gears!

**PETRO**  
*(quietly)*  
It also had a waiting list and no spare parts.

**BROK**  
Silence! The spirits are listening!

Above them, nothing is visible. Somewhere much higher, a satellite continues its scheduled pass, entirely unaware it has been accused of witchcraft.

**FADE OUT.**
