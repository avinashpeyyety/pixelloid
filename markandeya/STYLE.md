# STYLE — markandeya

Inherits the Mahabharata Ep 10 canvas bar (`../mahabharata/STYLE.md`), with a devotional palette:

- **Canvas:** native 3:2, ≥ 1536×1024 (consumer downloads arrive 1728×1152). Never 16:9 / 720p for plates.
- **Frame:** carved gold-and-lotus cartouche painted into the artwork (series spine `assets/spine/field-master.jpg`).
- **Camera:** heroic medium — named figures fill the frame. Not photoreal.
- **Look:** classic Indian painted-comic (Amar Chitra) art; warm hand-inked outlines; vermilion, saffron, peacock blue,
  leaf green, gold.
- **Tone:** devotional and child-appropriate. The Devi is radiant, serene, compassionate even in battle. Asuras are
  proud and fierce but never horror-styled. No blood, gore, wounds, skulls or severed heads; Kali's garland is red hibiscus.
- **Arms:** humans exactly two arms; the Devi exactly ten (symmetric); Vishnu and Brahma four; Kali four.
- **Light:** each plate declares dawn / day / morning / afternoon / sunset — never night unless the bible says so.
- **Music:** each episode names a Hindustani raga preset in `js/main.js` (`RAGA_PRESETS`); tanpura + bansuri, ducked
  under Orion.
